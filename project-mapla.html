the vision

Bilingual voice control & agentic OS tool execution.

Engineered to give developers and users a natural voice interface in both English and Tamil (ta-IN) that autonomously triggers local operating system tasks, command execution, and real-time telemetry search.

what I built

Feature-packed Agentic Voice Platform.

  • Bilingual Speech Recognition (STT) supporting English & Tamil (`ta-IN`) with live interim speech transcripts.
  • HTML5 Canvas reactive audio spectrum waveform orb giving real-time visual vocal feedback.
  • Autonomous Tool Engine: `web_search`, `execute_command`, `file_manager`, `system_monitor`, and `app_launcher`.
  • Dynamic Persona Selection: Toggle between **MAPLA AI** (friendly Tamil-bro persona) and **JARVIS Prime** (sleek tactical assistant).
