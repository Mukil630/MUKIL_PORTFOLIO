Botify WhatsApp SaaS

Open Live App ↗ Ask about it
the problem

Local businesses lacked automated customer support on WhatsApp.

Small retail businesses and service providers struggled to handle customer inquiries, order bookings, and service scheduling manually on WhatsApp without spending on expensive enterprise CRM tools.

what I built

A multi-tenant AI bot engine deployed on Railway.

  • Multi-tenant PostgreSQL database architecture isolating client bot instances and chat logs.
  • Groq LLM reasoning integration for instant natural language customer query resolution.
  • Automated appointment booking workflow with custom subscription management.
  • Deployed live on Railway PaaS infrastructure with automated CI/CD pipeline.
