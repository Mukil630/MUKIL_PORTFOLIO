the problem

Local bakers needed a fast way to browse & order tools.

A specialized bakery supply business in Karur needed a modern digital storefront so customers could explore hundreds of cake decorating tools and raw ingredients without manual phone calls.

what I built

Fast React storefront with automated WhatsApp cart checkout.

  • Dynamic inventory category filtering across baking tools, molds, and raw materials.
  • Automated WhatsApp order payload generator for frictionless checkout.
  • Optimized asset loading for high-resolution product photography.
